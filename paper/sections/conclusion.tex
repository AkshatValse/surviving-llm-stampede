\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

Prefix caching turns multi-turn agent serving into a thrashing workload: the cache that makes follow-up turns cheap belongs to sessions idle in tool calls, and admission reclaims it first. On SGLang the good state was metastable up to about twice the all-miss capacity: in a pre-registered test a burst moved the engine into an all-miss state that it did not leave, the no-burst and capped arms stayed good, and a timing-calibrated simulator had predicted this. Operators should measure the closed-population curve for the workload they serve, cap admitted sessions near its peak, and re-measure when the workload changes.
