\paragraph{Inputs and definitions.} Each cell's costs come from its frozen cell model (\fpath{analysis/theory/cells/*.yaml}): the
calibrated miss prefill of turn $k$ plus the all-miss decode share, chosen so that $1/\sum_kc_kG_k$ equals the first-principles
model's $\mu_b$. vLLM, the H200 and Llama take one speed factor fitted to their own E0a token rate, as in the paper's earlier
predictions. A run is stuck if its follow-up hit fraction over its window is below 0.2: $[5700,8700)$\,s for E2-protocol runs,
$[1500,3000)$\,s for unstable from-empty (E0b) runs. The composition statistic is the mean prompt of the turns completed in the
window; the gap slope is the least-squares slope, over the window, of the 300\,s medians of the gap between a turn's completion
and the same session's previous one; completions and backlog slope are over the window. Source:
\fpath{results/theory/icml/trap_check.md} and \fpath{.json}, produced by \fpath{analysis/theory/icml/run_check.py}; summary
numbers by \fpath{support.py} into \fpath{support.json}. An AI referee recomputed six statistics for 22 of the runs from the raw
files with independent code and matched them to five or more digits. The gap slope is regressed on the turn's completion time,
so its model-F constant is $\gamma/z=1-1/z$ (\fpath{analysis/theory/icml/gap_slope_completion_time.py}); the theory note's tables
compare it with $\gamma$, which is 1--13\% larger at these loads. The backlog column of the table below, as in
Table~\ref{tab:trap}, is the measured slope over model~F's $\alpha$, with the quasi-static $(\lambda-\lmiss)/\alpha$ in parentheses
(\fpath{analysis/theory/icml/backlog_over_alpha.py}, \fpath{results/theory/icml/backlog_over_alpha.json}).

The composition is within $-5.7$\% and $+6.6$\% and the gap-slope ratio between 0.63 and 1.37 (mean 1.03). Completions and
backlog slopes both run above the asymptotic values in 9 of the 13 runs. In model~F the two add up to the arrival rate, so this
may reflect window arrival rates above nominal (each is a single seed-0 run, with window arrivals 3.8--13.1\% above nominal) rather than the transient of a young trap.

\paragraph{E2-protocol runs beyond Table~\ref{tab:trap}.} The other stuck runs of the E2 protocol behave like the registered ones:
the SGLang 300k grid with the waiting-prefix refresh disabled (r0: composition 8{,}406--9{,}532 against 8{,}606 and 9{,}324), the
BASIN runs with bursts of 50, 60 and 100 sessions (8{,}372--9{,}272 against 8{,}606), SHED's 600\,s stop, TOOLSLOW's trapped run and
stock LPM past its 128-request fallback. Across all 57 runs the engine completes 0.90--1.21 times $D$, 1.055 on average; seven are
below $D$ (the late-forming BASIN traps of 50 and 60 sessions, seed~1, at 0.90 and 0.91; native 2.5~$\lref$ seed~1; H200 1.5~$\lref$
seed~1; three LPM runs). The measured token ratios are 0.95--1.12 against 1.01--1.05 from the composition alone; the excess is largest on the H200 and
the native pool, while the completion excess on vLLM and Llama is not explained by the token rate (the paper's checks of its
congestion assumptions, Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}).

\paragraph{The slope rule and the plans.} The client's constant-rate plan fixes each seed's base arrival times before any run, and
reproduces the measured window arrival rates exactly. Seed~1's plan delivers 0.8--8.9\% fewer arrivals than nominal over the window
in every cell and rate (5.8\% at 300k, 7.9\% on vLLM and 8.9\% on Llama at 1.35~$\lref$). The plug-in $\lambda_{\mathrm{window}}
(\text{plan})-D$ lies within 0.01\,/s of the measured backlog slope in 49 of the 57 runs and within 0.002\,/s in 18, with median
residual $-0.0022$\,/s because the engine completes slightly more than $D$. On the 36 runs of the five registered paired-test cells (30 registered runs and 6 earlier exploratory vLLM runs; 30 distinct
plans, the 12 vLLM runs being six plans run twice; plans of different cells share seeds) it falls on the measured side of the registered threshold in 33 runs and for 27
plans, against 23 for always predicting ``trapped''. The exceptions are H200 1.5~$\lref$ seed~1 (plug-in 0.04849 against a threshold
of 0.04847), Llama 1.65~$\lref$ seed~1 (above it by 0.00005) and Llama 1.35~$\lref$ seed~0 (0.0205 against a measured 0.0052).

\paragraph{Self-similarity.} By Theorem~\ref{thm:trap}(b), among completed sessions with similar $T$ that arrive in
$[2500,7000)$\,s, the sojourn should grow linearly in the arrival time with slope about $E[z^T-1]$. Measured over predicted
slope, per seed: 0.86--1.19 in every turn-count bin on the H200 at 600k (160--400 sessions per bin), 0.85--0.96 for short sessions on
the native pool at 2.5~$\lref$, 0.2--1.6 (noisy, 40--120 sessions per bin) on SGLang 300k, vLLM and Llama, and 0.1--0.8 for long
sessions at high $z$, where right-censoring at 8700\,s keeps only the faster ones. This check is descriptive, not a test.
