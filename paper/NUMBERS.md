# Numbers in the paper and where each comes from

One row per number in `paper/main.tex` and `paper/sections/*.tex` (abstract, body, figure and table captions, the statements after the conclusion, appendix). A value used in several places has one row that lists every place. "Where" uses section numbers of the built PDF (Abs = abstract, Avail / AI / Ack = the unnumbered "Code and data availability", "Use of AI tools" and "Acknowledgments" statements, A-G = appendices) and names figures and tables by their labels (F-e2 = `fig:e2`, T-fpval = `tab:fpval`, ...), so that a row does not change when a float moves. "Derived" means arithmetic on committed values; the arithmetic is given. Paths are relative to the repository root and resolve there (every backticked path was checked on 2026-10-31). A run that an analysis, a `meta.json` or a derived CSV names by its cluster path `results/agentload_*/<ts>` is stored under the pull directory of its jobs with the same final components, `results/nova/<pull>/results/agentload_*/<ts>` (the Stage 0, Stage C and E0a pulls have no inner `results/`: `results/nova/<pull>/agentload_*/<ts>`); the derived CSVs give both (`run_dir`, `run_path`). Commits are on `main` (or its ancestors) unless noted; the files of the 2026-10-30 revision that this file once marked "uncommitted" are committed on branch `r10/arxiv` (the extended `paper/derived/` files in baeae81, `provenance/` except `commit-map.tsv` in 3c755f3); job IDs are SLURM IDs on Nova; times are CDT (UTC-5). Section numbers are those of the 2026-10-31 build: 1 Introduction, 2 Background, 3 Quasi-static model (3.1-3.4), 4 First-principles model (4.1 model, 4.2 results, 4.3 heuristics, 4.4 validation, 4.5 weaknesses, 4.6 prior models), 5 Methodology (5.1-5.4), 6 Results (6.1 curve, 6.2 edge, 6.3 E2, 6.4 native E2, 6.5 vLLM, 6.6 sustaining variable, 6.7 blind predictions, 6.8 grid), 7 Frozen predictions, 8 Simulator studies (8.1 robustness, 8.2 mechanisms), 9 Diagnostic, 10 Related work, 11 Limitations, 12 Conclusion; Appendices A protocol, B registrations, C prediction rounds and timeline, D provenance, E proofs of the quasi-static results, F first-principles model, G supplementary results; tables T-fpval, T-e2, T-gapR, T-blind, T-frozen, T-stages, T-workload, T-rule, T-ledger, T-timeline, T-timeline-vllm, T-timeline-r11, T-prov; figures F-e2, F-fpmu, F-curve, F-edge, F-e2native, F-grid, F-fpscale, F-robust, F-mech, F-fixed. The "Where" entries of the rows written before 2026-10-31 were converted from the 2026-10-30 numbering (old 4.x to 5.x; 5.1-5.4 to 6.1-6.4; 5.5 to 6.7; 5.6 to 6.8; 5.7 to 6.5; 6 to 8.1; 7 to 8.2; 8-11 to 9-12; F1-F7 to F-e2, F-curve, F-edge, F-e2native, F-grid, F-robust, F-mech; T1-T8 to T-e2, T-blind, T-stages, T-workload, T-rule, T-timeline, T-timeline-vllm, T-prov), and rows whose sentences the revision moves to Appendix E (the (A3)/(A4) checks and the pin numbers of 3.4) or Appendix G (the B1n-B4n round, the LPM fallback analysis, the trace and mechanism details) name E or G; check them against the built PDF.
